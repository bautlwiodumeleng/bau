What is the Customer Management Portal?

The BAU Technologies Customer Management Portal provides businesses with a central place to organise and manage their customer information.

Instead of relying on scattered notebooks, spreadsheets, emails and separate files, businesses can keep important customer information organised within one portal.

Customer Management

Create and maintain detailed customer records containing important information such as:

  • Customer ID
  • Customer name
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Organisation
  • Business address
  • Customer notes

Customers can be searched, updated and managed whenever their information changes.

Customer Document Management

Keep documents associated with the correct customer record.

The portal supports common file types including:

  • PDF
  • Microsoft Word
  • Microsoft Excel
  • JPG and JPEG images
  • PNG images

Customer documents can be uploaded, stored and accessed when required.

Business Profile & Branding

Businesses can customise their profile with their own business information, including:

  • Business name
  • Business telephone
  • Business email
  • Business address
  • Website
  • Company logo

Your organisation information and logo can also appear on generated PDF reports.

Professional Reports

Customer information can be exported for business use.

  • PDF customer reports
  • Microsoft Excel exports
  • Organisation information in reports
  • Company branding and logo on PDF reports

This makes it easier to review, share and maintain professional customer information.

User Accounts

Each business user has their own account and can manage the customer information associated with that account.

The portal provides user login and password recovery functionality to help users securely access their accounts.

Who Is It For?

The Customer Management Portal is suitable for small and growing businesses that want a simple way to organise their customer information.

  • Small businesses
  • Growing businesses
  • Service providers
  • Consultants
  • Independent professionals
  • Businesses managing customer documents

Key Benefits

  • Keep customer information organised
  • Find customer information quickly
  • Keep customer documents together
  • Search and update customer records easily
  • Archive customers when necessary
  • Create professional PDF reports
  • Export information to Excel
  • Maintain your business profile and branding
