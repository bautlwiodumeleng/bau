What is the Customer Management Portal?

The BAU Technologies Customer Management Portal provides businesses with a central place to organise and manage their customer information.

Instead of relying on scattered notebooks, spreadsheets, emails and separate files, businesses can keep important customer information organised within one convenient portal.

The portal is designed to help businesses save time, improve organisation and manage customer information more efficiently.

Customer Management

Create and maintain detailed customer records containing important information such as:

  • Customer code
  • Customer name
  • Telephone number
  • Email address
  • Organisation
  • Business address
  • Customer notes

Customers can be searched, updated and managed whenever their information changes.

Each customer record can be identified using a unique Customer Code, making it easier to locate and manage individual customer records.

Customer Document Management

Keep documents associated with the correct customer record, helping you keep important business information organised.

The portal supports common file types including:

  • PDF
  • Microsoft Word
  • Microsoft Excel
  • JPG and JPEG images
  • PNG images

Customer documents can be uploaded and accessed when required from the relevant customer record.

Business Profile & Branding

Businesses can customise their profile with their own business information, including:

  • Business name
  • Business telephone
  • Business email
  • Business address
  • Website
  • Company logo

Your organisation information and logo can also appear on generated PDF reports, helping you maintain a professional business identity.

Professional Reports & Exports

Customer information can be exported for business use, record keeping and reporting.

  • PDF customer reports
  • Microsoft Excel exports
  • Organisation information in reports
  • Company branding and logo on PDF reports

These tools make it easier to review, share and maintain professional customer information.

User Accounts

Each business user has their own account and can manage the customer information associated with that account.

The portal provides user registration, login and password recovery functionality to help users manage access to their accounts.

Who Is It For?

The Customer Management Portal is suitable for small and growing businesses that want a simple and organised way to manage their customer information.

  • Small businesses
  • Growing businesses
  • Service providers
  • Consultants
  • Independent professionals
  • Businesses managing customer documents
